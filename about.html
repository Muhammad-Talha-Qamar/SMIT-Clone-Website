About SMIT

OUR FOUNDATION

Bridging the Digital Divide
Through Education

SMIT, an initiative of Saylani Welfare International Trust, is dedicated to providing high-quality, modern IT training to underprivileged and aspiring students across the globe. Our mission is simple: to equip the next generation with the coding and digital skills necessary to thrive in the 21st-century economy, irrespective of their financial background. We believe access to top-tier technological education is a right, not a privilege. By focusing intensely on practical application and job readiness, we ensure our graduates are prepared to compete in the global marketplace.
